The story behind the name

Life, defended.

Vitarmis (vih-TAR-miss)

Vita means life, and armis means “by arms” or strength. Together they mean “life, defended.”

We chose the name because it describes what good healthcare people do every day. They protect and care for others.

Our job is to look after them, by finding the right nurses, clinicians and leaders and helping them land where they'll do their best work. We're part of the Spartan Capital Group family, and we bring the same commitment to putting people first.
